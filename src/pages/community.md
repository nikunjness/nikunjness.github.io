Community building has run alongside my work for most of my career. I learned early on that bringing people together teaches you more than just showing up.

## Startup OG

I founded and host **Startup OG** (previously Entrepreneur's Cafe), a community for founders and builders. Through podcast conversations, meetups, and events, we talk with founders across SaaS, fintech, ecommerce, marketing, and technology about what building a company is really like. It's also how I keep learning from people who are a few steps ahead or taking a different path.

## Headstart Gujarat

I've been involved with and led **Headstart Gujarat**. Headstart is one of India's oldest and largest startup communities. My work there was about bringing founders, operators, and the wider ecosystem together through events and community programs.

## GDG Cloud Ahmedabad

I founded and organized **GDG Cloud Ahmedabad**, a Google Developer Group that brought together developers working with cloud technology.

## Open source, early on

Before any of that, open source was my first community. I took part in [Google Summer of Code 2013](/posts/grabbing-the-tag-of-gsocer/), met the open source community at [FOSSASIA](/posts/fossasia-2015-experience-unveiled/), and [mentored students in Google Code-in](/posts/google-code-in-with-fossasia/). I also served as Joint Secretary of the DA-IICT Alumni Association from 2017 to 2020.

## Recognition

I received the **Gujarat Entrepreneurship Award** at the Sayaji Startup Summit for contributions to the entrepreneurial ecosystem in Gujarat.
