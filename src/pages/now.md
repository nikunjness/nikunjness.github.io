## Building Snezzi

Most of my time goes into [Snezzi](https://snezzi.com), which I'm building with my co-founders.

More and more buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI what to buy. Brands want to be part of those answers instead of losing out to a competitor. Snezzi is an AI visibility platform. It tracks how a brand shows up across AI search, finds the gaps, and helps fix them through Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): prompt-based visibility tracking, content optimization, and CMS-native agents. For teams that would rather hand it off, we also run it as a done-for-you service covering content, technical SEO, backlinks, and authority building, and we measure the traffic and conversions it brings.

If you run marketing or growth and AI search is a blind spot for you, [let's talk](mailto:me@nikunjthakkar.com).
